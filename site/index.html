Workflow automation for freelancers, small businesses and households in the Philippines

Describe a routine task. Daloy drafts the automation.

Write what should happen in your own words, such as confirming a client's booking or logging an online order. The AI helper asks two or three questions, then shows the steps for you to check. Once you approve and test it, Daloy sends the emails, waits for approvals and keeps a record of each step.

How the AI helper will work

The demo shows this whole flow with scripted replies.

  1. Describe it. Type what should happen, or pick an example.
  2. Answer a few questions: what starts it, who should know, where it goes and whether anyone approves first. You tap an answer.
  3. Check the draft. The steps appear as plain sentences and as a diagram, with anything Daloy's checker thinks you should fix.
  4. Test it, then turn it on. Nothing is saved until you approve the draft, and nothing runs for real until you turn it on.

What it does

Track an office request

Staff fill in a form. Each request gets a reference. Your office receives a notification.

Purchase request with a named approver

Staff ask to buy something and get a reference. The person you name approves or rejects it in Daloy. Approved requests go to purchasing, from Outlook and with a Teams note if you connect them.

Reply to a website inquiry

Visitors fill in your inquiry form. They get a reply from your own email address, and your inbox gets the inquiry.

What it does not do

  • An automation runs in a line with branches. There are no loops and no parallel branches.
  • Gmail connects with an app password. There is no Google sign-in, so some work and school accounts cannot connect.
  • Google Sheets, Slack, Outlook and Teams are tested against local stand-ins only. They have not been checked against the real services.
  • AI steps and Build with AI drafts are tested against a fake model server. Their quality with a real model has not been measured.
  • The AI helper that asks questions before drafting is not built yet. The demo's replies are scripted.
  • Activity shows the latest 50 runs of each automation.
  • The diagram editor is built for a computer screen. A phone shows forms, approvals and activity.
  • There is no SMS and no Tagalog interface yet.
  • One workspace per user.

The full list is in the README.

What the demo does not do

The demo runs in your browser with made-up data. You describe a task or pick one of three examples (a client booking, an online-shop order, a household bill reminder), answer the helper's questions, check the draft and send a sample through it. The helper's replies are scripted for those three examples; anything else gets the nearest example and a note that the live helper is not ready. No AI model runs, nothing is saved, no email is sent, and you cannot sign in or connect your own apps there.

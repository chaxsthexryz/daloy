Workflow automation for small offices in the Philippines

Daloy runs the steps that follow a form.

Someone submits an office request, a purchase request or a website inquiry. Daloy gives it a reference, sends the emails, waits for an approval where you ask for one, and keeps a record of each step.

Daloy is in pilot. It is set up with you and hosted for you, and the first 14 days are free.

What it does

Track an office request

Staff fill in a form. Each request gets a reference. Your office receives a notification.

Purchase request with a named approver

Staff ask to buy something and get a reference. The person you name approves or rejects it in Daloy. Approved requests go to purchasing, from Outlook and with a Teams note if you connect them.

Reply to a website inquiry

Visitors fill in your inquiry form. They get a reply from your own email address, and your inbox gets the inquiry.

Each request shows the path it took

  1. Starts whenSomeone submits the form✓ Done

  • DoneEvery step finished.
  • WaitingIt is waiting for a time or for an approval.
  • Needs youA message may or may not have arrived. You check, then choose: it went through, send again, or stop.
  • ProblemA step failed. Retry runs that step once more.

What it does not do

  • An automation runs in a line with branches. There are no loops and no parallel branches.
  • Gmail connects with an app password. There is no Google sign-in, so some work and school accounts cannot connect.
  • Google Sheets, Slack, Outlook and Teams are tested against local stand-ins only. They have not been checked against the real services.
  • AI steps are tested against a fake model server. Their accuracy with a real model has not been measured.
  • Activity shows the latest 50 runs of each automation.
  • The diagram editor is built for a computer screen. A phone shows forms, approvals and activity.
  • There is no SMS and no Tagalog interface yet.
  • One workspace per user.

The full list is in the README.

What is planned

None of this is built yet. It is listed in the order it is likely to arrive, with no dates.

  1. A usage page that shows how much email and AI your office has used.
  2. SMS confirmations and reminders, as a paid add-on.
  3. A Tagalog interface and Tagalog emails.
  4. Starting an automation from an email, and a form you can place in your own website.
  5. A helper that explains in plain words why a step failed and what to do next.

What the demo does not do

The demo is one shared account. It cannot create API tokens, invite team members, save passwords or keys, or send real email. It resets on a schedule, so anything you make there is removed.

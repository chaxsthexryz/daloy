# Pilot UX audit (25 November 2026)

Tested via browser at `http://localhost:3000` across `1440x900` Light and Dark modes, English and Taglish, and `390x844` mobile as a non-technical boutique resort owner in the Philippines.

## Pilot verdict

A non-technical Philippine SMB owner would not survive setup or pay for kymavo today. Despite an inviting Sign In screen and thoughtful Philippine touches (GCash/QR Ph, PHP pricing, Taglish, Manila time), the app repeatedly promises ready-made, no-tech automation and then drops users into broken setups, contradictory rules, and developer internals. The exact moment to close the tab was on `#/w/11111313-f90b-4ab4-9e33-ec9514a3432f` (Booking with a GCash or QR Ph deposit) right after clicking **Try a sample** and **Publish** then **Turn on**: the side panel stated `Configuration checks: Every step has what it needs.` and `0 of 1 apps connected: Left: connect PayMongo`, yet **Try a sample** failed with `Test run failed: resource: must be one of this workspace's resources` (referring to a hidden `#/resources` page absent from the sidebar), and **Turn on** failed with `Publish failed: SMS is not set up on this server.` (an error the editor never flagged, for UniSMS, which is absent from `#/apps`). When **Build with AI** (`#/ai`) then charged monthly AI quota (`AI 2 of 20`) for two failed calls and linked to a broken `Check the AI model` route (`#/settings/ai`) that silently rendered Automations, trust broke down completely.

## Top 5 showstoppers (P0)

1. Contradictory and broken first-run path in the workflow editor (`#/w/:id`) [Functional Bug, UX/Comprehension]
   Opening the flagship Philippine template *Booking with a GCash or QR Ph deposit* (`#/new` to `#/w/:id`) hits three cascading blockers:
   - False green check: side panel states `Every step has what it needs.`, yet clicking **Try a sample** fails with HTTP 400 (`Test run failed: resource: must be one of this workspace's resources`). Meanwhile `Resources` (`#/resources`) sits inside `Settings > Your office` rather than `Bookings` or the sidebar.
   - Silent SMS blocker and PayMongo contradiction: the Library card says `"confirm in Receipts to review... Needs UniSMS"` without mentioning PayMongo. The editor banner says `0 of 1 apps connected` (`Left: connect PayMongo`) while ignoring UniSMS. The `Turn on` modal says `Payment: Manual QR Ph transfer, checked by a reviewer` (contradicting PayMongo) and lets you click **Turn on**, only to fail with HTTP 400: `Publish failed: SMS is not set up on this server.` (Plan and usage allocates `SMS messages: 0 of 0 this month`).
   - Dead Setup guide button: `Check the messages` is marked `Done` before any edit, and clicking `Open` does nothing.

2. Dead-end app connections and hidden Secrets flow (`#/apps`, `#/new`, `#/settings`) [UX/Comprehension, Functional Bug]
   *Book a Lalamove rider when an order is confirmed* instructs: `"Save lalamove_api_key and lalamove_api_secret in Secrets"`, and the editor variable picker tells users: `"Type {{secrets.name}} for a saved secret"`. Searching `Lalamove` or `GCash` in `#/apps` returns `"No app matches."`, UniSMS is absent from `#/apps`, and no `Secrets` link appears anywhere in `#/apps`, `#/settings`, `#/settings/business`, `#/team`, or `#/account`. For the 16 apps listed in `#/apps`, setup demands developer-portal credentials rather than OAuth: Gmail requires enabling 2FA and creating a 16-letter App Password; Messenger/Instagram requires `Meta App Review`, `Page Access Token`, `App Secret`, and `Verify Token`; Shopify requires `Shopify Dev Dashboard`, `access scopes read_orders and write_products`, `Client ID`, and `Client secret`; PayMongo requires `sk_test_...` and `whsk_...` webhook secrets.

3. AI features fail, charge quota on failure, and link to a dead route (`#/ai`, `#/w/:id`, `#/settings/ai`) [Functional Bug, Copy/Trust]
   On `#/ai`, submitting the resort prompt returned HTTP 502 with raw backend text `"The provider rejected the API key."`, still rendered premature `Build it now` and `Start over` buttons, logged the failed attempt as `In progress` in Conversation logs, and suggested an unrelated fallback template (`Track an office request`). Clicking the fallback link `Check the AI model` (`#/settings/ai`) hits a non-existent route that silently renders the Automations dashboard while highlighting `Settings` in the sidebar. On `#/w/:id` (*Resort concierge for Messenger & Instagram*), **Chat as your customer** promises `"Each message is a test run with the real AI model and uses one AI action"`, then returns `"Mock reply. Test runs without live calls do not call the model. The AI step failed (Publish the knowledge sheet (Business, Knowledge)...)"` and fires an automatic `POST /runs/:id/cancel` that fails with HTTP 409 (`"This run is failed, so it cannot be cancelled."`). Even though both AI interactions failed (`Mock reply` and `502`), workspace quota incremented from `AI 0 of 20 this month` to `AI 2 of 20 this month`.

4. Identity and trust leaks ("Daloy" branding, operator Gmail, external tunnel URL) (`#/`, `#/onboarding`, `#/settings/business`, `#/team`) [Copy/Trust, Functional Bug]
   Onboarding (`#/` banner and `#/onboarding`) repeatedly calls the product `"Daloy"` instead of `kymavo` (`"Three short questions, and Daloy makes a first draft you can test."`, `"Set up Daloy"`, `"What would you like Daloy to handle first?"`, `"Daloy gets the result"`). `#/settings/business` exposes an operator Gmail address directly in customer-facing copy: `"Replies to customers are sent from jbbalitos@gmail.com ,"` (with a stray space before the comma). `#/team` generates an invite link pointing to an external tunnel URL (`trycloudflare.com`) instead of the active origin, with no pending-invites list or revoke action.

5. Broken navigation, unusable Ask about a request, and hidden prerequisites (`#/activity/ask`, `#/bookings`, `#/new`, `#/apps`) [UX/Comprehension, Functional Bug]
   `#/activity/ask` ("Ask about a request") presents `Questions` and `"Type or pick a request to read what happened."`, yet has no request picker, no format hint, and returned HTTP 404 (`"No request with that reference in this office."`) for both a plain-English question and an actual run UUID copied from `#/activity`. Library previews (`#/new`) render inline at the top of the 44-card page with no auto-scroll and no `Escape` close. Sidebar active state desyncs across routes (`#/apps` left `Negosyo` marked active; `#/activity/ask` and `#/runs/:id` fail to highlight `Activity`). `Bookings` (`#/bookings`) has no link to `Resources` (`#/resources`), and `Knowledge` (`#/knowledge`) sits under `WORK` while the concierge error misdirects users to `(Business, Knowledge)`.

## UI and design system critique

### Spacing, alignment and layout
- Shopify radio buttons broken (`#/apps` > Shopify): both `<input type="radio">` inputs (`In the Dev Dashboard` and `In my store admin, before 2026`) render oversized/full-width and stacked vertically centered above their labels instead of inline beside them. (Visual/UI)
- Team invite banner crushed (`#/team`): after clicking `Create invite link`, the instruction text (`"Send this link to frontdesk@mariscove.example.com..."`) collapses into a ~60px vertical column of single words beside the unbroken invite URL. (Visual/UI)
- Canvas node clipping at default 50% Fit zoom (`#/w/:id`): node headers overlap and clip mid-word (`6 · PAYMONG check_paid`, `7 · KYMAVC still_unpaid`), body text truncates (`If: status from step check_paid ...`), and clicking `Fit` centers on middle steps while unmounting/hiding the Trigger node from view and DOM. (Visual/UI)
- Toast overlaps breadcrumb and title (`#/w/:id`): the top-center `"Created draft from template."` toast overlaps `Automations > Resort concierge...` and the `<h1>` heading. (Visual/UI)
- `Conversation logs` drawer z-index and faint delete button (`#/ai`): opening the drawer lets underlying `"Conversation details"` text bleed through during open, places the top-right `Conversation logs` button over the drawer's `×` close button, and renders the `Delete conversation` control as an almost invisible faint grey dot on the right edge of the card. (Visual/UI)
- Misplaced feedback messages (`#/settings/business`): clicking `Upload logo` without a file or clicking `Save colour` renders `"Choose a file first."` or `"Colour saved."` below the `"How your public form will look"` preview box instead of next to the clicked button. (Visual/UI)
- Mismatched input heights and unstyled native file pickers (`#/onboarding`, `#/settings/business`, `#/knowledge`, `#/resources`): native `<input type="file">` controls render raw and untranslated (`Choose File | No file chosen` even in Taglish); in `#/knowledge`, the `Charged` `<select>` is shorter than adjacent numeric inputs; in `#/resources`, placeholder `"Room type or label (optional)"` clips mid-word as `"Room type or label (opti"`. (Visual/UI)
- Double focus ring on Library search (`#/new`): focusing `"Search"` draws a blue focus outline inset inside an outer border box. (Visual/UI)
- Taglish layout crush in Editor Setup Guide (`#/w/:id`): longer Taglish labels squeeze the `Gabay sa pag-setup` left column into 1-word vertical stacks (`Ikonekta / ang / mga / app`, `Subukan / gamit / ang / sample`) and force `"Gagawin pa"` to wrap across 2 lines. (Visual/UI)

### Typography, hierarchy and buttons
- Competing primary CTAs: on `#/w/:id`, `Publish` is bright blue even when `0 of 1 apps connected` and 4 Setup Guide items are `To do`, and on mobile it competes with the top-bar blue `New` button. On `#/knowledge`, both `Publish` and `Add an entry` render as competing bright blue primary buttons whenever unpublished edits exist. (Visual/UI)
- Fragmented Save actions (`#/settings/business`): three separate save buttons (`Save business details` primary, `Upload logo` secondary, `Save colour` secondary) across two side-by-side cards make it easy to edit colour/logo and lose changes by clicking only the primary blue button. (UX/Comprehension)
- Unsegmented editor toolbar (`#/w/:id`): `Test run`, `Explain`, `Share`, `Save`, and `Publish` sit in one flat horizontal row with uniform spacing, and the right-panel collapse chevron (`>`) floats awkwardly on the seam beside the dark connection banner. (Visual/UI)
- Redundant status pills (`#/w/:id`): header displays `Draft` chip directly next to `Version 1, draft`. (Visual/UI)
- Semantic heading inconsistency: `#/ai` uses `<h2>` (`Turn your ideas into automations`) with no `<h1>` until after a prompt is submitted; `#/w/:id` lacks a `<main>` landmark. (Visual/UI)

### Icons and logos
- Unlabeled `Needs` icons on Library cards (`#/new`): template cards list `Needs` followed only by small icon chips with no text labels or tooltips, leaving users unable to identify PayMongo or the bare blue `U` circle (UniSMS). (UX/Comprehension)
- Missing brand icon for UniSMS (`#/new`, `#/w/:id`): renders as a placeholder blue `U` circle while other apps have vector logos. (Visual/UI)
- Messenger icon omits Instagram (`#/apps`): `Facebook Messenger & Instagram` displays only the Messenger logo (`/logos/messenger.svg`). (Visual/UI)
- Harsh white icon boxes in Dark mode (`#/apps`): all 16 `22x22` SVGs sit inside bright white squares against `#14171f` cards. (Visual/UI)

### Light and dark mode contrast
- Semantic colour bug in both Light and Dark modes (`#/`): under the `Problems` summary card, `0 all healthy` (and `0 maayos lahat` in Taglish) renders in bright red/pink, making a zero-error state look like an urgent failure. `0 all clear` under `Needs you` renders in warning olive/amber. (Visual/UI)
- Low-contrast disabled buttons in Dark mode (`#/apps`, `#/knowledge`): in Dark mode on PayMongo (`I-save ang key`, `Ikonekta ang PayMongo`) and Knowledge (disabled `I-publish`), dark muted text on dark backgrounds is hard to read. (Visual/UI)
- Low-contrast progress bar in Dark banner (`#/w/:id`): the `0 of 1 apps connected` progress bar track is a barely visible dark-grey sliver on a near-black card. (Visual/UI)

### Breadcrumbs and navigation
- Inconsistent breadcrumbs and active states: `#/w/:id` uses `Automations > Booking...`, `#/account` uses `Settings › Account` (different separator glyph and tight sidebar pill), `#/activity/ask` uses `Activity › Ask` (while sidebar fails to highlight `Activity`), and `#/settings/ai` highlights `Settings` while silently rendering Automations. (Visual/UI)
- Terminology split across nav and CTAs: the product alternates between `Automations` (sidebar, `#/`), `Workflows` (onboarding Step 3, Library subtitle, Bookings empty state), `Test run` (top toolbar) vs `Try a sample` (right panel), and `Publish` (editor) vs `Turn on` (Setup guide and modal) vs `Turn it on` (`#/ai` English) vs `Buksan na` (`#/ai` Taglish). (UX/Comprehension)

### Mobile layout (390x844)
- Card text overflow in Workflow Editor List view (`#/w/:id`): on `2 · BOOKING book`, `"Resource: Who or what to book from the form"` spills past the right border of the card and overlaps the `↑` Move Up button. (Visual/UI)
- Excessive header stack (`#/w/:id`): over 70% of the `390x844` screen height is occupied by stacked headers (`kymavo + New`, 2-line wrapped breadcrumb with orphan `>`, 2-line title, draft badge, 3 tabs, 5 toolbar buttons, dark connection banner, `Undo/Redo`, `Diagram/List`) before the first trigger card appears. (Visual/UI)
- Bottom `Quick` bar occludes drawer and page footers: opening the mobile `Menu` drawer hides `Account` and `Sign out` behind the fixed bottom `Quick` bar until scrolled; the `Menu` label in the bottom bar renders in a smaller/lighter font weight than `Automations`, `Activity`, and `AI`. (Visual/UI)
- Unreadable Diagram mode on mobile (`#/w/:id`): switching from the default List view to Diagram renders miniature nodes with clipped labels (`6 · PAYMONC check_paid`). (Visual/UI)
